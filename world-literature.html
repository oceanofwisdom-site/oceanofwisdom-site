World Literature & Classical Texts

Original introductions to influential works from India and the wider world.

This collection brings together literature, sacred texts, philosophy, science and political thought. Entries will distinguish traditional attribution from what can be established about composition, compilation, surviving manuscripts and later reception.

Indian texts and literary traditions

Four Vedas

Rigveda, Yajurveda, Samaveda and Atharvaveda: their different collections, ritual settings, recensions, oral transmission and historical study.

Ramayana traditions

Valmiki Ramayana and major retellings across languages: Kamba Ramayanam, Ramcharitmanas, Adhyatma Ramayanam, Krittivasi Ramayan, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Assamese, Odia, Marathi and other traditions, alongside Southeast Asian adaptations.

Mahabharata & Bhagavad Gita

The layered epic and the Gita's dialogue on action, knowledge, devotion and disciplined insight.

Puranic literature

Bhagavata Purana, Vishnu Purana, Shiva Purana and Garuda Purana: narrative, cosmology, theology, pilgrimage and textual variation.

Philosophical works

Ashtavakra Gita and major works attributed to Adi Shankaracharya, with care over attribution, commentary traditions and dating.

Kautilya's Arthashastra

Statecraft, administration, revenue, trade, agriculture, law, diplomacy, intelligence and defence. Authorship and composition date remain scholarly questions.

Tamil grammar & Sangam literature

Tolkappiyam, Ettuthokai and Pattuppattu: grammar, poetics, landscapes and the social worlds represented in classical Tamil poetry.

Kalidasa

Abhijnanashakuntalam, Vikramorvashiyam, Malavikagnimitram, Raghuvamsha, Kumarasambhava, Meghaduta and Ritusamhara.

Aryabhata

Aryabhatiya and its mathematical and astronomical contributions in the history of Indian science.

World religious, philosophical & literary works

Qur'an

Structure, revelation, compilation, recitation, interpretation and its place in Islamic intellectual and devotional life.

Bible

The diverse library of Jewish and Christian scriptures: canons, genres, languages, translation history and interpretive traditions.

Avesta

The Zoroastrian sacred textual corpus, including the Gathas. “Book of Ahura Mazda” is not a precise bibliographic title; the collection is properly introduced as the Avesta.

Other world classics

Further entries will cover Mesopotamian, Egyptian, Greek, Roman, Persian, Chinese, African and Indigenous American literary and intellectual works.

Copyright policy: Ancient works may be public domain, but modern translations, critical editions, notes, introductions, photographs and digital transcriptions may remain protected. Ocean of Wisdom will publish original summaries and explanations, use only lawful quotations, credit editions consulted and link to authorised or openly licensed texts. No modern translation will be copied wholesale.
