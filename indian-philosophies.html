Explore the diverse philosophical and religious traditions that developed in the Indian subcontinent.

A broad family of traditions shaped by Vedic and post-Vedic literature, ritual, philosophical inquiry, devotional movements and varied paths of practice.

Charvaka / Lokayata

An Indian materialist and sceptical current whose surviving doctrines are known mainly through later accounts by philosophical opponents. Charvaka sources are fragmentary; the school is commonly associated with prioritising perception as a means of knowledge and challenging ritual claims, afterlife doctrines and unseen karmic mechanisms. Traditional attribution to Brihaspati is legendary rather than securely documented. A careful study distinguishes the school's reported positions from caricatures in polemical sources.

Sikhism

A distinct tradition founded in the Punjab through the teachings of Guru Nanak and the Sikh Gurus, centred on devotion, equality and service.
