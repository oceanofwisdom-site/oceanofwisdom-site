About Ocean of Wisdom

Ocean of Wisdom is an independent educational project created to make ideas and sources from Indian and world knowledge traditions easier to explore. It brings together original explanations, historical context, philosophical questions, literature, research references and interactive learning tools.

Our purpose

We aim to encourage thoughtful inquiry rather than prescribe belief. Readers should be able to compare traditions without flattening their differences, consult sources directly, and distinguish what is documented from what is interpreted or believed.

What we publish

  • Original educational explanations and essays.
  • Introductions to philosophical and religious traditions, with cultural context.
  • References to primary texts, open-access scholarship and institutional collections.
  • Learning tools and research aids, with limitations stated where relevant.

Editorial independence

Ocean of Wisdom is not a university, religious authority or substitute for specialist scholarship. Content is educational and may be revised as sources, evidence and interpretations are reviewed. We do not claim ownership of the traditions, source texts or scholarship discussed here.

Editorial commitment

We own the original explanations and presentation we create. We respect the rights of authors, translators, artists, communities and institutions, and we link to source material rather than treating a link as permission to reproduce it.

For details, read our research methodology and Editorial, Sources & Rights Policy.
