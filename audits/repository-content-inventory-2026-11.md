### P3 — Targeted improvement rather than bulk expansion

- **Classical language profiles (11 pages):** current visible text is roughly 367–444 words each. Add language-specific, verified material—historical stages, script development, literary periods, grammar resources and open editions. Replace repeated generic meta descriptions with unique, accurate descriptions. Do not mechanically add identical paragraphs.
- **Religion/tradition profiles:** Buddhism (405), Jainism (445), Sikhism (403) have more initial depth than the shortest profiles, but still need source, chronology, internal diversity and cross-link checks.
- **Library category pages:** 498–876 words; prioritise distinct curated sources and examples rather than padding.
- **Ancient Indian tools (651) and Ancient Indian Wisdom (928):** improve tool-by-tool descriptions, citations, functional status and links to relevant original explanations.
- **Forgotten Books (637):** add specific open editions/manuscript catalogues and explain access conditions.
- **Vedic Rishis (432):** already has contextual sections on Lopamudra, Ashtavakra and Shankara, with appropriate caveats; improve primary-source access rather than add bulk. Added a VedaWeb research link.
- **Wisdom Feed (397) and Explore (209):** keep these as concise discovery/index pages. Improve article summaries, internal links and navigation rather than padding them with general prose.
- **Lapis Lazuli (1,209), architectural marvels (2,777), water engineering (1,790), world civilisations (1,830):** depth is not the primary issue; audit evidence quality, attribution, internal links and unique metadata.
- **Long-form question and comparative essays (1,200–2,400 words):** do not expand indiscriminately. Check argument structure, references, balance, originality and duplication.
- **Sanatana Dharma (17,857 words):** do not add bulk. Review structure, navigation, factual sourcing, duplication, page performance and whether the content should be better segmented with internal anchors.

## 4. Homepage redesign scope

The homepage will be rebuilt as a **tool-first, knowledge-rich discovery page**, not a long directory:
1. Preserve the current Ocean of Wisdom branding, banner, colour palette and typography.
2. Add a compact responsive dropdown navigation: Explore; Free Tools; Knowledge; Languages & Literature; Research; About.
3. Put a prominent site-wide search directly below the hero/navigation.
4. Feature only verified working tools near the top, with concise descriptions and clear free/external labels.
5. Add a small set of knowledge highlights, then the six fundamental questions, language/literature and research entry points.
6. Keep all existing routes accessible through search, dropdowns or curated cards; do not delete content.
7. Search must index the site's own pages and tools, show direct links and useful excerpts, handle no-results gracefully, and provide a clearly labelled external fallback. Do not imply it can answer arbitrary questions absent from the site's content.

## 5. SEO and reference audit findings from the initial sample

- Several content pages use generic template descriptions (notably Ancient Cosmology, Lost Knowledge, Sacred Geography and Ancient World Wisdom).
- Classical language profiles repeat near-identical descriptions; descriptions should be language-specific.
- Religion pages such as Christianity, Islam, Taoism and Zoroastrianism need richer subject-specific titles/descriptions and balanced historical framing.
- Verify every cited source opens, is genuinely free to read, and supports the exact nearby claim. A link to a general search page is not a substitute for a primary source or paper.
- Distinguish primary sources, translations, scholarly interpretation, living religious tradition and contested hypotheses.
- Check internal links for meaningful contextual placement, not merely a generic list of links.

7. Deploy only after all gates pass; then live-test the deployed site.
