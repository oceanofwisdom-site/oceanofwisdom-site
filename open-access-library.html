Open Access Research & Literature Library

A curated doorway to free books, scholarly papers, theses, manuscripts, primary texts and university learning resources.

Our access promise: every link below is selected because the destination provides free access to at least a meaningful part of its collection. Some repositories contain both free and restricted records; some allow reading but not reuse. Always check the licence on the individual item before copying, adapting or republishing it.

This page is a research gateway, not a claim that every item in every linked database is openly licensed. Where possible, prefer the individual full-text PDF, HTML, repository record or publisher page explicitly marked “Open Access”.

How to use these sources well

  1. Start with a reference article or scholarly overview to understand the field and its terminology.
  2. Follow its bibliography to primary sources, peer-reviewed papers, theses or critical editions.
  3. Prefer full-text repository copies and publisher pages marked Open Access; do not assume that a search-result PDF is lawful to reuse.
  4. Record author, title, publisher or journal, year, DOI or stable URL, page numbers and access date.
  5. Separate primary evidence, scholarly interpretation, traditional accounts and modern popular claims.
  6. For Ocean of Wisdom, write original summaries and cite sources; quote only what is lawful and necessary.

Free to read does not automatically mean public domain or Creative Commons. Reuse rights must be checked at the item level. Some repositories also contain restricted, embargoed or metadata-only records.
