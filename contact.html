Contact & Corrections

We welcome specific reports about factual errors, broken or inaccessible sources, transliteration or translation issues, accessibility problems and copyright or licensing concerns.

How to report an issue

  1. Open the relevant page and copy its full URL.
  2. Describe the exact passage, link or feature that needs attention.
  3. For a factual or translation correction, include a reliable source or explain the evidence supporting the correction.
  4. For a rights concern, identify the material and provide relevant ownership, licence or permission information where available.

Submit a public issue

Use the project's GitHub issue tracker to report a problem or suggest a correction. Issues are public, so do not include passwords, private correspondence, personal addresses or other sensitive information.

Open Ocean of Wisdom issue tracker ↗

There is currently no verified private contact address or newsletter subscription service configured on the website. We have therefore not added a pretend contact form or newsletter box that would collect data without a working delivery and privacy process.

Reports are reviewed as capacity permits. A submission does not guarantee a particular response time or outcome.
