Language and literary heritage

प्राकृत (a family of Middle Indo-Aryan literary languages)

Script and writing

Multiple scripts across regions and periods; no single universal Prakrit alphabet. Study letter shapes alongside inscriptions, manuscripts, printing history and contemporary digital typography.

📜

Literature and knowledge

Jain canonical literature; Hāla's Gāhā Sattasaī; plays using Prakrit dialogue; inscriptions.

🎉

Festivals and living traditions

No single Prakrit-specific festival; associated traditions include Jain observances and regional literary commemorations. These are cultural traditions of communities and regions, not properties of a language alone.

🧵

Clothing and crafts

No single Prakrit-specific dress; cultural settings vary by region and period. Clothing is diverse and changes by region, occasion, community and period.
