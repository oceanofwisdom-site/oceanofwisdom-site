कखगघङचछजझञटठडढणतथदधनपफबभमयरलवशषसहळ

Special conjuncts: क्ष, ज्ञ.

This follows the Marathi Language Department's published character-table order. School conventions and linguistic descriptions may group the vowel signs and nasal/breath marks differently. क्ष and ज्ञ are special conjunct forms, not independent basic consonants. Marathi is written in Devanagari today; the Modi script also has an important historical role in Marathi documents.

मराठी

Script and writing

Devanagari (historically Modi script also used). Study letter shapes alongside inscriptions, manuscripts, printing history and contemporary digital typography.

📜

Literature and knowledge

Dnyaneshwari; Eknathi Bhagwat; Tukaram's abhangas; Mahanubhava texts; modern Marathi literature.

🎉

Festivals and living traditions

Ganeshotsav; Gudi Padwa; Wari pilgrimage traditions. These are cultural traditions of communities and regions, not properties of a language alone.

🧵

Clothing and crafts

Nauvari saree, pheta and regional clothing traditions. Clothing is diverse and changes by region, occasion, community and period.
