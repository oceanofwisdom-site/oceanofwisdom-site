Language and literary heritage

Script and writing

Telugu script. Study letter shapes alongside inscriptions, manuscripts, printing history and contemporary digital typography.

📜

Literature and knowledge

Nannaya's Āndhra Mahābhāratam; Tikkana; Errana; Pōtana; Śrīnātha; Vemana.

🎉

Festivals and living traditions

Ugadi; Sankranti; Bathukamma; Bonalu. These are cultural traditions of communities and regions, not properties of a language alone.

🧵

Clothing and crafts

Traditional dhoti/panche and saree styles; regional weaving traditions. Clothing is diverse and changes by region, occasion, community and period.
