Language and literary heritage

বাংলা

Script and writing

Eastern Nagari / Bengali script. Study letter shapes alongside inscriptions, manuscripts, printing history and contemporary digital typography.

📜

Literature and knowledge

Charyapada; Mangal-kavya; Chaitanya literature; Bankimchandra; Rabindranath Tagore; Kazi Nazrul Islam.

🎉

Festivals and living traditions

Durga Puja; Poila Boishakh; Saraswati Puja. These are cultural traditions of communities and regions, not properties of a language alone.

🧵

Clothing and crafts

Tant and Jamdani sarees; dhoti and regional dress traditions. Clothing is diverse and changes by region, occasion, community and period.
