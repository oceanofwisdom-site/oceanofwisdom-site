Language and literary heritage

മലയാളം

Script and writing

Malayalam script. Study letter shapes alongside inscriptions, manuscripts, printing history and contemporary digital typography.

📜

Literature and knowledge

Rāmacaritam; Eḻuttacchan's Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇam; Kṛṣṇagāthā; modern Malayalam poetry and prose.

🎉

Festivals and living traditions

Onam; Vishu; Thrissur Pooram; Theyyam traditions. These are cultural traditions of communities and regions, not properties of a language alone.

🧵

Clothing and crafts

Mundu, settu mundu and kasavu saree traditions, with regional variation. Clothing is diverse and changes by region, occasion, community and period.
