Language and literary heritage

संस्कृतम्

Script and writing

Devanagari (also many historical scripts). Study letter shapes alongside inscriptions, manuscripts, printing history and contemporary digital typography.

📜

Literature and knowledge

Vedas; Upanishads; Mahābhārata; Rāmāyaṇa; Kālidāsa; classical kāvya and śāstra.

🎉

Festivals and living traditions

Saraswati Puja; Sanskrit Day; regional festivals with Sanskrit liturgical traditions. These are cultural traditions of communities and regions, not properties of a language alone.

🧵

Clothing and crafts

No single Sanskrit-specific dress; traditions vary by region and community. Clothing is diverse and changes by region, occasion, community and period.
