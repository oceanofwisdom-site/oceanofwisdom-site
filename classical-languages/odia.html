Language and literary heritage

ଓଡ଼ିଆ

Script and writing

Odia script. Study letter shapes alongside inscriptions, manuscripts, printing history and contemporary digital typography.

📜

Literature and knowledge

Sarala Mahabharata; Jagannath Das's Bhagabata; Upendra Bhanja; modern Odia literature.

🎉

Festivals and living traditions

Rath Yatra; Raja Parba; Nuakhai. These are cultural traditions of communities and regions, not properties of a language alone.

🧵

Clothing and crafts

Sambalpuri and other regional handloom traditions; dhoti and saree styles. Clothing is diverse and changes by region, occasion, community and period.
