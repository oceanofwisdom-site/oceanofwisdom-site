11-Language Translator & Analyser

One input. Eleven language panels. Evidence, grammar and uncertainty made visible.

Accuracy notice: This first release is a curated lexicon demonstrator, not yet an unrestricted machine-translation engine. It returns only explicitly curated equivalents; it will not invent a sentence translation when no verified entry exists. Full grammar-aware translation requires validated dictionaries, corpora and language-specific analysers.

Results are restricted to the curated concept list. Native-script forms and listed transliterations are accepted; this is not unrestricted sentence translation.

What a full analysis will add

Morphology

Case, number, gender, person, tense, aspect, mood, inflection and derivation where relevant.

Syntax and compounds

Word order, agreement, sandhi, compounds, particles and clause structure.

Evidence and confidence

Dictionary headwords, source passages, variant readings, provenance and a clear uncertainty label.

The tool will not treat semantic equivalents as cognates. For each word, historical relationship and translation equivalence are separate questions.
