Language and literary heritage

Script and writing

Kannada script. Study letter shapes alongside inscriptions, manuscripts, printing history and contemporary digital typography.

📜

Literature and knowledge

Kavirājamārga; Pampa; Ranna; Ponna; Vachana literature; Kuvempu.

🎉

Festivals and living traditions

Mysuru Dasara; Ugadi; Karnataka Rajyೋತ್ಸವ. These are cultural traditions of communities and regions, not properties of a language alone.

🧵

Clothing and crafts

Mysore silk, Ilkal sarees and regional attire; panche/dhoti traditions. Clothing is diverse and changes by region, occasion, community and period.
