Language and literary heritage

Script and writing

Tamil script. Study letter shapes alongside inscriptions, manuscripts, printing history and contemporary digital typography.

📜

Literature and knowledge

Tolkāppiyam; Eṭṭuttokai; Pattuppāṭṭu; Tirukkuṟaḷ; Cilappatikāram; Maṇimēkalai.

🎉

Festivals and living traditions

Pongal; Tamil New Year; Karthigai Deepam. These are cultural traditions of communities and regions, not properties of a language alone.

🧵

Clothing and crafts

Veshti and saree traditions; regional variations. Clothing is diverse and changes by region, occasion, community and period.
