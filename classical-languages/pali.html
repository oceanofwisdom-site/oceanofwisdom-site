Language and literary heritage

पालि / pāḷi

Script and writing

Historically written in several scripts; Devanagari is one modern convention. Study letter shapes alongside inscriptions, manuscripts, printing history and contemporary digital typography.

📜

Literature and knowledge

Tipiṭaka: Vinaya, Sutta and Abhidhamma; Dhammapada; commentarial literature.

🎉

Festivals and living traditions

Vesak and Buddhist observances in communities using Pali liturgy. These are cultural traditions of communities and regions, not properties of a language alone.

🧵

Clothing and crafts

No single Pali-specific dress; Buddhist monastic robes vary across traditions. Clothing is diverse and changes by region, occasion, community and period.
