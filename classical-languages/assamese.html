The Assamese script belongs to the Eastern Nagari tradition but has distinct Assamese letter forms and orthographic conventions. The letters ৰ (Assamese ra) and ৱ (Assamese wa) are especially important distinctions from standard Bengali usage. This practical modern-script inventory is not a complete phonological analysis; conjuncts, vowel signs, pronunciation and historical forms require separate study.

Literature and knowledge

Caryāpada connections; Sankardev's Borgeet and Ankiya Naat; Buranjis; modern Assamese literature.

🎉

Festivals and living traditions

Bihu (Rongali, Kongali, Bhogali); Ali-Aye-Ligang in regional context. These are cultural traditions of communities and regions, not properties of a language alone.

🧵

Clothing and crafts

Mekhela chador, riha and gamosa traditions. Clothing is diverse and changes by region, occasion, community and period.
