Open Access Research & Free Literature

A curated research gateway: primary texts, scholarly books, academic papers, manuscripts, journals and learning resources that readers can access without a subscription.

How to use this page: begin with a subject collection, then follow its links to the original institution, repository or publisher. Where possible, links go directly to full text or to a catalogue that identifies free full-text copies. Availability and terms can change, so check the individual item's access and licence statement.

This page is a discovery guide, not a claim that every item in every linked database is free. We prioritise fully open repositories and public-domain collections, and clearly distinguish them from catalogues that may contain both free and restricted records.

Our access, citation and copyright standard

  • Direct access first: prefer full-text pages or downloadable files hosted by universities, public institutions, scholarly publishers and recognised repositories.
  • No paywall presented as free: where a catalogue includes restricted items, say so clearly and link to the free-access filter or repository copy when available.
  • Licence-aware use: free reading does not automatically permit copying, republishing, translation or commercial reuse. Check the individual licence and rights statement.
  • Primary sources and scholarship: distinguish original texts, translations, critical editions, peer-reviewed articles, preprints, institutional explainers and popular summaries.
  • Transparent citations: identify author, title, institution or journal, date, stable URL or DOI, and access date when an individual work is cited in an Ocean of Wisdom article.
  • Editorial independence: our explanations and presentation are original; source material is credited and never copied wholesale.

Access note: this is a curated starting collection, not a guarantee that every external service is permanently available in every country. Links should be reviewed periodically, and any item that becomes paywalled or unavailable should be replaced by a lawful free alternative.
