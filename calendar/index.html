Historical research note: 10 January 5114 BCE (astronomical year -5113), 12:30 PM, Ayodhya is one published proposed astronomical reconstruction of Rama’s birth, not a historically established or universally accepted date. This calendar tests the sky against that candidate; it does not assume the candidate is correct. Published sources also differ in BCE/astronomical-year and Julian/Gregorian notation, and the historical date remains disputed.
Extended research range: 13,000 BCE–2526 CE. This is an astronomical exploration window, not a claim that the Vedic texts date to 13,000 BCE. Swiss Ephemeris is designed for long-range calculations, but the available browser build and individual calculations have different limits. Historical sunrise/sunset and visibility are estimates where Earth-rotation or model uncertainty is significant.

Sunrise, Rahu Kaal & traditional day periods

Rahu Kaal is a traditional weekday division of daylight, not an astronomical force or a scientifically demonstrated bad-time interval.
