Islam — Prophet Muhammad

Origins and historical setting

Islam emerged in seventh-century Arabia. Muslims regard Muhammad as the final prophet in a line that includes Abraham, Moses and Jesus. The Qur’an is understood as revelation from God received by Muhammad.

Central teachings

Tawhid, the oneness of God, is central. Islam emphasises worship, mercy, justice, honesty, charity and responsibility toward neighbours and those in need. The Five Pillars shape Muslim religious life.

Texts, practices and communities

The Qur’an is Islam’s foundational scripture. The Sunnah and hadith literature also inform belief and practice. Sunni and Shia communities, and schools within them, have developed diverse legal, theological and spiritual traditions.

Legacy and continuing life

Islamic civilisation contributed to mathematics, medicine, astronomy, geography, architecture and philosophy through exchanges among scholars and cultures. Its history includes many regions and varied Muslim communities.
