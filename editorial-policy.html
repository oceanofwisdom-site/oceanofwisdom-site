Editorial, Sources & Rights Policy

Ocean of Wisdom aims to publish original explanations, research notes and educational presentation while respecting the rights of authors, translators, artists, communities and institutions.

Our rule: We own the wording and presentation we create. We do not claim ownership of source texts, translations, photographs, recordings, illustrations, research articles or other third-party material.

Original writing

Explanations are drafted in original language. Facts, dates, methods and ideas may be discussed in our own words; distinctive expression from another author is not copied. Quotations, where used, must be brief, necessary, accurately attributed and checked for rights and applicable law.

Research references are not reuse licences

A link to a book, article, archive, museum, university or heritage institution is provided so readers can consult the source. A link does not grant permission to reproduce, translate, adapt, download and republish or commercially exploit its content. Each image, recording, translation and document requires its own rights check.

Reuse standard

  • CC0 or verified public-domain material may be reused after checking the specific item and jurisdiction.
  • CC BY material may be reused only while meeting its attribution and licence conditions.
  • Other Creative Commons licences must be checked for commercial use, adaptations and share-alike obligations.
  • All other third-party material is reference-only unless written permission or another valid legal basis is documented.
  • We do not assume that an ancient original makes a modern translation, edition, photograph or recording public domain.

Historical and cultural accuracy

We distinguish primary evidence, scholarly interpretation, religious tradition, disputed claims and speculation. Traditions are not treated as interchangeable merely because a theme appears similar. Dates, quotations, attributions and translations must be qualified when evidence is uncertain. We aim to describe living traditions respectfully and avoid presenting one interpretation as the only view.

Corrections and rights concerns

If you are a rights holder or reader who identifies a possible error or rights concern, please contact the site owner with the page URL, the material in question and relevant ownership or licence information. We will review and, where appropriate, correct, replace or remove the material.

Legal note

This policy describes our editorial practice and is not legal advice or a guarantee that every existing page has completed a legal review. Copyright rules vary by jurisdiction and by the specific work. In India, consult the Copyright Act, 1957 and official guidance from the Copyright Office.
