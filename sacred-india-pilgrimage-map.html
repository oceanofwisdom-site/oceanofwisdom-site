Sacred India: Pilgrimage Sites Across Faiths

From the Himalayan shrines of the north to the island coasts of the south, India's landscape is woven with places of pilgrimage. This living map gathers major sites of six great traditions — Hindu, Muslim, Sikh, Christian, Jain and Buddhist — with how to reach them, what to do, their rituals, and the stories told about them.

Tap a marker to jump to its guide, or use the filters to explore by faith or by region. Coverage spans Kashmir to Kanyakumari and Dwarka to the Andamans, including Lakshadweep. This is a curated selection of major sites, not a complete list — more will be added over time.

Hindu Uttarakhand · North India

Kedarnath

How to reach

Nearest airport Dehradun (Jolly Grant); railheads at Rishikesh and Haridwar. Road runs to Gaurikund, from where the shrine is a roughly 16–18 km Himalayan trek on foot, pony or palanquin, with helicopter services from Phata and Guptkashi.

What to do & see

Take darshan of the conical rock lingam, attend the morning and evening aarti, and sit awhile in the thin, cold mountain air that pilgrims describe as part of the experience.

Rituals & practices

Abhishekam of the lingam and aarti are the core rites. The shrine is snowbound in winter and opens only from around April–May to November, between Akshaya Tritiya and Bhai Dooj.

What it is famous for

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Shiva and among the highest, it anchors both the Char Dham circuit of Uttarakhand and the Panch Kedar.

Folklore & mythology

By tradition the Pandavas, seeking to be absolved of the bloodshed at Kurukshetra, pursued Shiva for his blessing. Shiva, reluctant, took the form of a bull and dived into the ground, leaving his hump at Kedarnath while other parts are said to have surfaced at the four other Panch Kedar shrines.

Hindu Uttarakhand · North India

Badrinath

How to reach

Dehradun airport and Rishikesh/Haridwar railheads, then road via Joshimath. The town sits beside the Alaknanda river below Nar and Narayan peaks.

What to do & see

Take darshan of the black-stone Badrinarayan image, and bathe in the Tapt Kund hot spring beside the river before entering the temple.

Rituals & practices

A dip in the Tapt Kund precedes darshan. Like Kedarnath, the shrine closes in winter and reopens around late April or May.

What it is famous for

One of the four great Char Dham of India and a seat re-established by the philosopher Adi Shankara, it is among the most revered Vishnu shrines.

Folklore & mythology

The name is linked to a tale in which Vishnu meditated here in deep austerity while Lakshmi, to shelter him from the snow, took the form of a badri (jujube) tree — so the place became Badri-nath, lord of the badri.

Hindu Jammu & Kashmir · North India

Amarnath Cave

How to reach

Fly to Srinagar; the yatra sets out from Pahalgam (Chandanwari) or the shorter Baltal route, on foot, pony or helicopter. The cave lies at about 3,888 m and is reached only in the short summer season.

What to do & see

View the naturally forming ice lingam deep in the cave, which waxes and wanes through the season.

Rituals & practices

Darshan of the ice lingam during the Shravani Mela (July–August) is the whole purpose of the arduous trek.

What it is famous for

The cave shelters a stalagmite of ice that devotees honour as a self-formed lingam of Shiva.

Folklore & mythology

Tradition holds that Shiva chose this remote cave to tell Parvati the secret of immortality, the Amar Katha. A pair of doves is said to have overheard the eternal words, and pilgrims who glimpse them count it a blessing.

Hindu Jammu & Kashmir · North India

Mata Vaishno Devi

How to reach

Base town Katra is linked by rail and by Jammu airport. The cave shrine in the Trikuta hills is about 12–13 km uphill, now served by battery cars, a ropeway and helicopters as well as the old walking track.

What to do & see

Climb to the holy cave and take darshan of the three natural rock pindis that represent the Goddess in her threefold form.

Rituals & practices

Pilgrims chant to the Mata on the climb and complete the journey with darshan of the pindis; many also visit the Bhairon temple above.

What it is famous for

One of the most visited Shakti shrines in India, drawing millions each year.

Folklore & mythology

The Goddess, in the form of a young girl, is said to have fled the tantric Bhairon Nath and sheltered in the cave. After a long pursuit she finally struck him down, his head falling where the Bhairon temple now stands. Repenting, he was forgiven, and pilgrims hold the journey incomplete without visiting him too.

Hindu Uttarakhand · North India

Haridwar & Rishikesh

How to reach

Dehradun airport; Haridwar is a major railway junction with Rishikesh close by. Both sit where the Ganga leaves the mountains for the plains.

What to do & see

Join the evening Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri in Haridwar, where lamps are floated on the river; in Rishikesh, cross the footbridges and visit the ashrams that made it a world centre of yoga.

Rituals & practices

A dip in the Ganga at Har Ki Pauri and the lamp-lit aarti are the signature rites. Haridwar is one of the four cities that host the Kumbh Mela.

What it is famous for

Haridwar is a gateway to the Char Dham and a Kumbh city; Rishikesh is widely called the yoga capital of the world.

Folklore & mythology

Haridwar is counted among the places where drops of amrit, the nectar of immortality, are said to have fallen, which is why the Kumbh returns here.

Hindu Uttar Pradesh · North India

Varanasi (Kashi)

How to reach

Lal Bahadur Shastri airport and a major railway station serve the city on the Ganga's western bank.

What to do & see

Take a dawn boat along the ghats, attend the grand evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh, and visit the Kashi Vishwanath temple.

Rituals & practices

Bathing in the Ganga, offering the evening aarti, and darshan at the Vishwanath Jyotirlinga are central. Manikarnika and Harishchandra are among the city's cremation ghats.

What it is famous for

One of the world's oldest continuously lived-in cities and, for Hindus, the city of Shiva where dying is believed to grant liberation.

Folklore & mythology

Tradition says Kashi rests on the trident of Shiva and stands outside ordinary time, untouched even when the world dissolves — so those who die here are said to receive Kashi-labh, the gift of release.

Hindu Uttar Pradesh · North India

Ayodhya

How to reach

The new Maharishi Valmiki International Airport and a rail link serve the city on the Sarayu river.

What to do & see

Take darshan at the Ram Mandir, consecrated in January 2024, walk the Ram ki Paidi ghats and attend the Sarayu aarti.

Rituals & practices

Darshan of Rama and the riverside aarti are the main observances; Diwali, marking Rama's return, is celebrated on a vast scale with rows of lamps.

What it is famous for

Revered as the birthplace of Rama and the ancient capital of the Ikshvaku kings.

Folklore & mythology

The Ramayana remembers Ayodhya as Rama's capital, to which he returned after fourteen years of exile and the defeat of Ravana — a homecoming lit by lamps that tradition recalls as the first Diwali.

Hindu Uttar Pradesh · North India

Mathura & Vrindavan

How to reach

Nearest airports at Agra and Delhi; Mathura is on the main railway line. The twin towns sit along the Yamuna.

What to do & see

Visit the Krishna Janmabhoomi at Mathura and the many temples of Vrindavan, and time a trip for Janmashtami or the exuberant Holi.

Rituals & practices

Temple darshan, parikrama of the sacred groves, and festival celebration are central. Barsana's Lathmar Holi is especially famous.

What it is famous for

Mathura is honoured as Krishna's birthplace and Vrindavan as the scene of his boyhood and the raslila.

Folklore & mythology

Tradition tells that Krishna was born in a Mathura prison to Devaki and Vasudeva under the shadow of the tyrant Kamsa, then carried across the Yamuna to be raised among the cowherds of Gokul and Vrindavan, where the gopis danced with him in the moonlit raslila.

Hindu Uttar Pradesh · North India

Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam)

How to reach

Prayagraj has an airport and a major railway junction; the Sangam is a short ride from the city centre.

What to do & see

Take a boat to the Sangam, the meeting of the Ganga and Yamuna, and bathe at the confluence.

Rituals & practices

A ritual dip at the Sangam is the chief act, above all during the Magh Mela and the great Kumbh and Maha Kumbh gatherings.

What it is famous for

Site of the world's largest periodic gathering of pilgrims, where the Ganga and Yamuna meet.

Folklore & mythology

A third river, the Saraswati, is believed to join the two unseen, and Prayagraj is counted among the places where amrit fell during the churning of the cosmic ocean.

Hindu Madhya Pradesh · Central India

Ujjain (Mahakaleshwar)

How to reach

Nearest airport Indore, with Ujjain on the railway network, beside the Shipra river.

What to do & see

Attend the pre-dawn Bhasma Aarti at Mahakaleshwar, one of the most striking temple rituals in India.

Rituals & practices

The Bhasma Aarti, in which the lingam is honoured with sacred ash, is unique to this shrine. Ujjain hosts the Simhastha Kumbh.

What it is famous for

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas, where Shiva is worshipped as Mahakal, the lord of time and death.

Folklore & mythology

Ujjain is remembered as a seat of kings and astronomers, long treated as a prime meridian of Indian astronomy, and among the sites touched by the falling drops of amrit.

Hindu Rajasthan · West India

Pushkar

How to reach

Nearest airport and railhead at Ajmer, a short drive away; the town rings a desert lake.

What to do & see

Bathe at the lake ghats, visit the rare Brahma temple, and, in autumn, see the famous Pushkar camel fair.

Rituals & practices

A dip in Pushkar Lake and darshan at the Brahma temple are the main rites; Kartik Purnima draws the largest crowds.

What it is famous for

Home to one of the very few temples in India dedicated to Brahma the creator.

Folklore & mythology

Tradition says Brahma let a lotus fall to earth, and where its petals touched down the lake appeared. A later account holds that his consort, displeased at a hurried rite, decreed that Brahma be worshipped here and almost nowhere else.

Hindu Gujarat · West India

Dwarka

How to reach

Nearest airport Jamnagar; Dwarka is on the railway line at Gujarat's western edge, by the Arabian Sea.

What to do & see

Take darshan at the towering Dwarkadhish temple and cross to Bet Dwarka island by boat.

Rituals & practices

Temple darshan and the raising of the great flag over the spire several times a day are notable; Janmashtami is the chief festival.

What it is famous for

One of the four Char Dham and among the seven holy cities, honoured as Krishna's own kingdom.

Folklore & mythology

Dwarka is remembered as the golden city Krishna built by the sea, said in tradition to have been reclaimed by the ocean after he departed the world — a story that continues to draw interest to the waters offshore.

Hindu Gujarat · West India

Somnath

How to reach

Nearest airports at Diu and Rajkot; railhead at Veraval, close to the seafront temple.

What to do & see

Take darshan of the Jyotirlinga and watch the sea crash below the temple, which faces an unbroken stretch of ocean.

Rituals & practices

Darshan and aarti are central; an evening sound-and-light presentation recounts the temple's much-rebuilt history.

What it is famous for

Honoured as the first among the twelve Jyotirlingas, destroyed and rebuilt many times across the centuries.

Folklore & mythology

By tradition the moon god Soma raised the first shrine here in gold to atone for a curse that had dimmed his light, and by Shiva's grace his waning and waxing were set in balance — which is why the place is called Somnath, lord of the moon.

Hindu Maharashtra · West India

Nashik & Trimbakeshwar

How to reach

Nashik has an airport and railway station; Trimbakeshwar lies a short drive away near the source of the Godavari.

What to do & see

Bathe at the Godavari ghats in Nashik, take darshan at the Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga, and visit Panchavati, linked to the Ramayana.

Rituals & practices

Riverside bathing and darshan are central; Nashik is one of the four Kumbh Mela cities.

What it is famous for

Home to the Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga and to Panchavati, remembered as part of Sita and Rama's forest exile.

Folklore & mythology

Panchavati is where tradition places Rama, Sita and Lakshmana during their exile, and the episode of Lakshmana and Surpanakha; nearby Trimbakeshwar is honoured as the source of the Godavari, the 'Ganga of the South'.

Hindu Maharashtra · West India

Pandharpur

How to reach

Nearest airports at Pune and Solapur; Pandharpur is on the railway, beside the Chandrabhaga river.

What to do & see

Take darshan of Vitthal and Rukmini, and, if you can, join the Ashadhi Ekadashi wari, when pilgrims walk for days carrying the palkhis of the saint-poets.

Rituals & practices

The wari pilgrimage, singing abhangs along the way, and darshan of Vitthal are the heart of the Warkari tradition.

What it is famous for

The spiritual home of Maharashtra's Warkari movement and its beloved saint-poets.

Folklore & mythology

Tradition tells of Vitthal standing patiently on a brick at the call of his devotee Pundalik, who asked the Lord to wait while he finished serving his aged parents — and so the deity is shown standing, hands on hips, upon that brick.

Hindu Maharashtra · West India

Shirdi (Sai Baba)

How to reach

Shirdi has its own airport and the Sainagar Shirdi railway station.

What to do & see

Take darshan at the Samadhi Mandir and visit Dwarakamai, the old mosque where Sai Baba lived.

Rituals & practices

The daily cycle of aartis and darshan at the samadhi shrine draws large crowds through the day and week.

What it is famous for

The home of Sai Baba of Shirdi, revered across communities as a saint and teacher.

Folklore & mythology

Sai Baba, whose origins remain unknown, lived simply in a mosque he called Dwarakamai and taught 'Sabka Malik Ek' — that there is one master of all — drawing Hindu and Muslim devotees alike, as he still does.

Hindu Andhra Pradesh · South India

Tirupati (Tirumala)

How to reach

Tirupati has an airport and railway station; the hilltop temple at Tirumala is reached by road or by a traditional footpath of stone steps.

What to do & see

Take darshan of Venkateswara, offer a tonsure (hair offering) if you have vowed one, and receive the celebrated Tirupati laddu as prasad.

Rituals & practices

Queuing for darshan, head-tonsuring as a vow, and the laddu prasad are the signature observances of this immense temple.

What it is famous for

Among the most visited and richly endowed temples in the world.

Folklore & mythology

A much-loved tale holds that Venkateswara borrowed heavily from Kubera, lord of wealth, to meet the costs of his marriage to Padmavati, and that devotees' offerings help repay that endless debt — a story often told to explain the temple's great treasury.

Hindu Tamil Nadu · South India

Madurai (Meenakshi)

How to reach

Madurai has an airport and a major railway junction; the temple stands at the heart of the old city.

What to do & see

Wander the towering, sculpture-covered gopurams and the thousand-pillar hall, and take darshan of Meenakshi and Sundareswarar.

Rituals & practices

Nightly the image of Sundareswarar is carried to Meenakshi's chamber; the annual Chithirai festival re-enacts their celestial wedding.

What it is famous for

A vast temple-city dedicated to the goddess Meenakshi and her consort, an icon of South Indian temple architecture.

Folklore & mythology

Legend tells that Meenakshi was born to a Pandya king with three breasts and a prophecy that the third would vanish when she met her lord. On meeting Shiva as Sundareswarar it did, and their marriage — celebrated each year at Chithirai — is remembered as a union of the divine with the city itself.

Hindu Tamil Nadu · South India

Rameswaram

How to reach

Nearest airport Madurai; Rameswaram is connected by rail across the Pamban bridge to the island.

What to do & see

Take darshan at Ramanathaswamy, walk its famed long pillared corridors, and bathe at the twenty-two theerthams (holy wells) within the temple.

Rituals & practices

Bathing in the sacred wells and darshan of the Jyotirlinga are central; many pilgrims come to perform rites for ancestors.

What it is famous for

One of the Char Dham and home to the Ramanathaswamy Jyotirlinga, with one of the longest temple corridors in India.

Folklore & mythology

Tradition says Rama worshipped Shiva here to be cleansed of the sin of war against Ravana, and that Sita fashioned a lingam from sand when the image sent for had not arrived — honoured to this day alongside it. From this shore, the epic places the bridge to Lanka.

Hindu Tamil Nadu · South India

Kanyakumari

How to reach

Nearest airport Thiruvananthapuram; Kanyakumari is the southern terminus of several railway lines.

What to do & see

Take darshan at the Kumari Amman temple, watch sunrise and sunset over three seas, and take the ferry to the Vivekananda Rock Memorial.

Rituals & practices

Darshan of the virgin goddess and a dip at the three-seas confluence are the main rites.

What it is famous for

The southern tip of the Indian mainland, where the Bay of Bengal, Arabian Sea and Indian Ocean meet.

Folklore & mythology

The goddess Kanya is said to have undertaken penance to wed Shiva. When the marriage was thwarted before dawn, the rice and lentils prepared for the feast are believed to have turned to stone — which devotees link to the distinctive grains of sand on the shore — and she remained the virgin guardian of the land's end.

Hindu Kerala · South India

Sabarimala

How to reach

Nearest airports Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram; the forest shrine is reached by road to Pamba and then a trek uphill.

What to do & see

Complete the climb to the shrine of Ayyappa after the traditional forty-one-day vratham, carrying the irumudi bundle up the eighteen holy steps.

Rituals & practices

A 41-day discipline of austerity, black or blue attire, and the chant 'Swamiye Saranam Ayyappa' precede the pilgrimage, which peaks in the Mandala–Makaravilakku season.

What it is famous for

A forest hill shrine drawing millions of Ayyappa devotees each season.

Folklore & mythology

Ayyappa is honoured as the son of Hari and Hara — of Vishnu in the form of Mohini and of Shiva — raised by the king of Pandalam, who grew to subdue the demoness Mahishi. The eighteen sacred steps and the long forest vow are bound up with his story.

Hindu Kerala · South India

Guruvayur

How to reach

Nearest airport Kochi; Guruvayur has its own railway station in central Kerala.

What to do & see

Take darshan of Guruvayurappan in the traditional dress the temple requires, and see the temple elephants nearby.

Rituals & practices

Early-morning nirmalya darshan, strict dress codes and offerings of the Lord's favourite foods mark temple life here.

What it is famous for

A great Krishna temple often called the Dwarka of the South.

Folklore & mythology

Tradition says the image was installed by Guru, the teacher of the gods, together with Vayu the wind — and from Guru and Vayu the place took its name, Guruvayur.

Hindu Odisha · East India

Puri (Jagannath)

How to reach

Nearest airport Bhubaneswar; Puri is a railway terminus on the Odisha coast.

What to do & see

Take darshan of Jagannath, Balabhadra and Subhadra, and, in summer, witness the Rath Yatra when the deities ride enormous chariots.

Rituals & practices

Daily offerings of the Mahaprasad and the annual Rath Yatra are central; the deities are periodically renewed in the Nabakalebara.

What it is famous for

One of the Char Dham and the home of the world-famous chariot festival.

Folklore & mythology

Tradition tells that the wooden images were being carved by a divine craftsman on condition he not be disturbed; interrupted too soon, he left them with their striking unfinished forms — which devotees cherish as they are. Each summer the deities journey by chariot to the Gundicha temple and back.

Hindu Assam · Northeast India

Kamakhya

How to reach

Guwahati has an airport and major railway station; the temple crowns the Nilachal hill above the city.

What to do & see

Take darshan at the Shakti Peetha and, if you come in June, observe the Ambubachi Mela.

Rituals & practices

Worship at the yoni-shaped stone and the Ambubachi Mela, which marks the goddess's believed annual cycle, are distinctive to this tantric shrine.

What it is famous for

One of the most important Shakti Peethas and a major centre of tantric worship.

Folklore & mythology

According to the Shakti Peetha tradition, when Sati's body was borne grieving through the heavens and dismembered, her yoni fell upon this hill — and so the goddess is worshipped here as the source of creative power, the earth itself said to renew each year.

Hindu West Bengal · East India

Dakshineswar & Kalighat

How to reach

Both lie within Kolkata, served by its airport, railway and metro; Kalighat is in the south of the city and Dakshineswar by the river to the north.

What to do & see

Visit the Dakshineswar Kali temple, linked to the saint Ramakrishna, and the ancient Kalighat Kali shrine.

Rituals & practices

Darshan and offerings to the Mother Kali are central; both temples are busiest around Kali Puja.

What it is famous for

Dakshineswar is bound to Ramakrishna's life and visions; Kalighat is one of the Shakti Peethas.

Folklore & mythology

Kalighat is remembered in tradition as the place where a part of Sati — her toes — fell to earth, giving the goddess her home in the city; at Dakshineswar, the mystic Ramakrishna is said to have experienced the Mother as a living presence.

Muslim Rajasthan · West India

Ajmer Sharif Dargah

How to reach

Nearest airports Jaipur and Kishangarh; Ajmer is well connected by rail.

What to do & see

Offer a chadar at the tomb of Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti and sit to hear the qawwals sing in the courtyard.

Rituals & practices

Pilgrims tie threads, offer flowers and a cloth covering, and attend the annual Urs commemorating the saint. Qawwali devotional singing is central.

What it is famous for

The shrine of the great Sufi saint Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti, venerated across communities.

Folklore & mythology

Known affectionately as Gharib Nawaz, 'benefactor of the poor', the Khwaja is remembered for a life of service and generosity, and many stories tell of rulers and ordinary people alike walking humbly to his door.

Muslim Delhi · North India

Jama Masjid & Nizamuddin Dargah

How to reach

Delhi's airport, railway and metro reach both the old city and Nizamuddin.

What to do & see

See the great courtyard of the Jama Masjid, built under Shah Jahan, and visit the Nizamuddin Dargah, where qawwals sing on Thursday evenings.

Rituals & practices

Congregational prayer at the mosque and devotional qawwali and offerings at the dargah are the main observances.

What it is famous for

One of India's largest mosques and the resting place of the Sufi saint Nizamuddin Auliya, with the poet Amir Khusrau nearby.

Folklore & mythology

A famous saying attributed to Nizamuddin Auliya — 'Delhi is still far off' — is remembered in connection with a sultan who set out against him but died before reaching the city, taken by his devotees as a mark of the saint's grace.

Muslim Maharashtra · West India

Haji Ali Dargah

How to reach

In the heart of Mumbai, reached by road from anywhere in the city; the shrine stands on an islet off Worli.

What to do & see

Walk the causeway out to the white shrine at low tide and take in the sea on all sides.

Rituals & practices

Pilgrims cross the tidal causeway to offer prayers at the tomb, timing their visit to the tides.

What it is famous for

A striking island shrine in the Arabian Sea, reached by a causeway that the sea covers at high tide.

Folklore & mythology

Tradition holds that the saint Pir Haji Ali died while on pilgrimage to Mecca, and that his casket returned across the waters to rest on these rocks — where the dargah was raised around it.

Muslim Telangana · South India

Mecca Masjid

How to reach

In Hyderabad, near the Charminar, reached by road and metro from across the city.

What to do & see

Visit one of India's oldest and largest mosques, beside the landmark Charminar.

Rituals & practices

Congregational prayer is central; the mosque is busiest at Friday prayers and during Ramadan.

What it is famous for

A great Qutb Shahi–era mosque in the old city of Hyderabad.

Folklore & mythology

The mosque takes its name from a tradition that bricks brought from Mecca were set into its central arch, linking the Hyderabad shrine to the holy city.

Muslim Jammu & Kashmir · North India

Hazratbal Shrine

How to reach

In Srinagar, on the shore of Dal Lake, reached by road from the city and its airport.

What to do & see

Visit the white marble shrine by the lake, especially on the days when the relic is displayed.

Rituals & practices

The shrine preserves a relic revered as a hair of the Prophet Muhammad, shown to gathered devotees on special occasions.

What it is famous for

Kashmir's most revered Muslim shrine, beautifully set on Dal Lake.

Folklore & mythology

The relic, the Moi-e-Muqaddas, is the heart of the shrine's devotion, and its display on holy days draws vast, emotional crowds to the lakeside.

Muslim Tamil Nadu · South India

Nagore Dargah

How to reach

Nearest town Nagapattinam on the Tamil Nadu coast, reached by road and rail; nearest airport Tiruchirappalli.

What to do & see

Visit the dargah of the Sufi saint Hazrat Syed Shahul Hameed, with its tall towers, and see the shared Hindu-Muslim devotion around it.

Rituals & practices

Offerings, the lighting of lamps and the annual Kanduri festival mark the shrine, which coastal communities honour for safe seas.

What it is famous for

A major Sufi shrine on the Coromandel coast, revered across communities.

Folklore & mythology

The saint is remembered for acts of healing and kindness, and fishing families along the coast have long sought his blessing for protection on the water.

Sikh Punjab · North India

Golden Temple (Harmandir Sahib)

How to reach

Amritsar has an international airport and a major railway station; the temple is in the old city.

What to do & see

Walk the marble parikrama around the sacred pool, take darshan in the gilded central shrine, and share a meal in the langar, the vast free community kitchen.

Rituals & practices

Listening to the continuous singing of the Guru Granth Sahib, bathing in the Amrit Sarovar and eating in the langar are central.

What it is famous for

The holiest shrine of Sikhism, its gold-leafed sanctum set in the middle of a sacred pool.

Folklore & mythology

The shrine was built with doors on all four sides — a deliberate sign that it is open to people of every direction and background. Destroyed in the eighteenth century and rebuilt, it was later adorned with gold in the time of Maharaja Ranjit Singh.

Sikh Punjab · North India

Anandpur Sahib

How to reach

Nearest airport Chandigarh; Anandpur Sahib has a railway station in the Shivalik foothills.

What to do & see

Visit Takht Kesgarh Sahib and, if you come at Hola Mohalla, watch the Nihang displays of horsemanship and martial skill.

Rituals & practices

Darshan at the Takht and the festival of Hola Mohalla, with its martial traditions, are central here.

What it is famous for

The birthplace of the Khalsa, one of the five Takhts, or seats of Sikh authority.

Folklore & mythology

It was here, on Baisakhi in 1699, that Guru Gobind Singh is remembered to have called for the devoted, tested their resolve, and founded the Khalsa — the collective body of initiated Sikhs.

Sikh Uttarakhand · North India

Hemkund Sahib

How to reach

Dehradun airport and Rishikesh/Haridwar railheads, then road to Govindghat and a steep trek via Ghangaria. The shrine sits by a glacial lake at about 4,300 m and opens only in summer.

What to do & see

Climb to the star-shaped gurudwara beside the high mountain lake and take a dip in its icy waters.

Rituals & practices

The arduous high-altitude trek, prayer at the gurudwara and a dip in the glacial lake are the pilgrimage's heart.

What it is famous for

One of the highest Sikh shrines, set beside a glacial lake ringed by peaks.

Folklore & mythology

The place is linked in Sikh tradition to the meditation, in a former life, of Guru Gobind Singh beside a lake of seven peaks — which devotees identify with this remote and beautiful tarn.

Sikh Bihar · East India

Takht Sri Patna Sahib

How to reach

Patna has an airport and major railway station; the shrine is in the eastern part of the city.

What to do & see

Visit the gurudwara marking Guru Gobind Singh's birthplace and see relics associated with his early life.

Rituals & practices

Darshan and prayer at the Takht are central; the Guru's birth anniversary is a major gathering.

What it is famous for

The birthplace of Guru Gobind Singh and one of the five Takhts.

Folklore & mythology

Patna remembers the childhood of the tenth Guru, and the gurudwara preserves the memory and relics of his early years in the city.

Sikh Maharashtra · West India

Takht Sachkhand Hazur Sahib

How to reach

Nearest airport Nanded; the city is on the railway network in central-western India.

What to do & see

Visit the grand gurudwara at Nanded on the banks of the Godavari.

Rituals & practices

Darshan, prayer and the observance of the Guru's final days mark this Takht.

What it is famous for

The place where Guru Gobind Singh spent his last days, and one of the five Takhts.

Folklore & mythology

It was at Nanded, by tradition, that the tenth Guru declared the Guru Granth Sahib the eternal Guru of the Sikhs before passing from the world in 1708.

Sikh Delhi · North India

Gurudwara Bangla Sahib

How to reach

In central Delhi, close to Connaught Place, reached by road and metro.

What to do & see

Take darshan, share in the langar, and sit by the sarovar whose water pilgrims regard as healing.

Rituals & practices

Prayer, langar and the drawing of water from the sacred tank are central here.

What it is famous for

A major Delhi gurudwara linked to Guru Har Krishan.

Folklore & mythology

The shrine recalls the boy-Guru Har Krishan, who is remembered for tending the sick during an epidemic in the city — and the sarovar's water is cherished by devotees for its believed healing power.

Christian Tamil Nadu · South India

Basilica of Our Lady of Good Health, Velankanni

How to reach

Nearest airport Tiruchirappalli; the coastal town is reached by road and a nearby railway station.

What to do & see

Visit the basilica by the sea and, if you come at the September feast, join one of the largest Christian pilgrimages in India.

Rituals & practices

Pilgrims light candles, make offerings in thanksgiving, and process during the eleven-day feast each September.

What it is famous for

A great Marian shrine on the Coromandel coast, often called the Lourdes of the East, drawing people of many faiths.

Folklore & mythology

Local tradition tells of the Virgin Mary appearing to a shepherd boy and to a buttermilk vendor, and of Portuguese sailors saved from a storm who built a chapel in gratitude — seeds of the shrine that grew here.

Christian Goa · West India

Basilica of Bom Jesus

How to reach

Nearest airport Goa (Dabolim/Mopa); Old Goa is a short drive from Panaji.

What to do & see

See the baroque basilica and the tomb of St Francis Xavier, whose relics are exposed for veneration once roughly every ten years.

Rituals & practices

Veneration of the relics of St Francis Xavier, especially during the periodic exposition, draws pilgrims from around the world.

What it is famous for

A UNESCO World Heritage church holding the remains of St Francis Xavier.

Folklore & mythology

The body of the saint, carried back to Goa after his death at sea off the China coast, is venerated as remarkably preserved, and its rare public exposition remains a major event in the Catholic world.

Christian Tamil Nadu · South India

San Thome Basilica & St Thomas Mount

How to reach

Both are within Chennai, served by its airport, railway and metro.

What to do & see

Visit the basilica built over the tomb of St Thomas and climb St Thomas Mount, traditionally the place of his martyrdom.

Rituals & practices

Prayer at the apostle's tomb and veneration of the ancient cross on the Mount are central.

What it is famous for

One of the few churches in the world built over the tomb of an apostle.

Folklore & mythology

Christian tradition in India holds that the apostle Thomas came to the subcontinent in the first century, preached along the coast and was martyred near Chennai — and the basilica and the Mount keep his memory.

Christian Kerala · South India

Malayattoor St Thomas Church

How to reach

Nearest airport Kochi; the hill shrine is reached by road and then a climb from the base.

What to do & see

Climb the hill to the church and see the cross and the rock devotees associate with the apostle's prayer.

Rituals & practices

The hill climb, especially during the Malayattoor Perunnal pilgrimage after Easter, is the central act of devotion.

What it is famous for

A hilltop shrine in Kerala linked by tradition to St Thomas.

Folklore & mythology

Local tradition says the apostle Thomas prayed on this hill, leaving marks on the rock, and pilgrims climb barefoot in his memory, calling on him as they ascend.

Jain Jharkhand · East India

Shikharji (Parasnath)

How to reach

Nearest railhead Parasnath on the Delhi–Kolkata line; nearest airports Ranchi and Patna, then road to Madhuban at the base.

What to do & see

Undertake the barefoot circuit over Parasnath Hill, visiting the many small shrines (tonks) along the long ridge walk.

Rituals & practices

The strenuous barefoot hill pilgrimage, often begun before dawn, is the central observance; the hill is treated with great reverence and care.

What it is famous for

The holiest Jain pilgrimage, where many of the twenty-four Tirthankaras are believed to have attained liberation.

Folklore & mythology

Jain tradition holds that twenty of the twenty-four Tirthankaras achieved moksha on this hill, making the whole ridge sacred ground — which is why pilgrims walk it barefoot and guard its sanctity so carefully.

Jain Gujarat · West India

Palitana (Shatrunjaya)

How to reach

Nearest airport Bhavnagar; Palitana town is reached by road and rail, with the temple hill rising above it.

What to do & see

Climb the thousands of steps up Shatrunjaya hill to its crowning cluster of hundreds of marble temples; by custom no one remains on the hill overnight.

Rituals & practices

The dawn climb, performed barefoot and often fasting, and darshan at the Adinatha temple are central. The hill is left to the deities by night.

What it is famous for

A hill crowned with hundreds of intricately carved temples, among the most sacred Jain sites.

Folklore & mythology

Shatrunjaya is associated in tradition with Rishabhanatha, the first Tirthankara, and generations of devotees and merchants have built and rebuilt its temples as acts of devotion, filling the summit with marble shrines.

Jain Rajasthan · West India

Dilwara Temples, Mount Abu

How to reach

Nearest railhead Abu Road; nearest airport Udaipur, then road up to the hill station of Mount Abu.

What to do & see

Admire the astonishingly fine marble carving of the Dilwara temples, especially the ceilings and pillars of the Vimal Vasahi and Luna Vasahi.

Rituals & practices

Quiet darshan and reverence are the norm; the temples are revered as much for devotion as for their artistry.

What it is famous for

Jain marble temples of the 11th–13th centuries renowned for the delicacy of their carving.

Folklore & mythology

The temples are remembered as offerings by wealthy patrons who are said to have spared no effort or expense, and the lace-like marble is often told of as the work of craftsmen paid by the weight of the marble dust they removed.

Jain Rajasthan · West India

Ranakpur Jain Temple

How to reach

Nearest airport Udaipur; reached by road through the Aravalli hills, with Falna the nearest railhead.

What to do & see

Walk among the 1,444 carved pillars of the Adinatha temple, said to be no two alike, and watch the light move through the marble halls.

Rituals & practices

Darshan at the Chaumukha (four-faced) Adinatha shrine is central in this serene hill-valley setting.

What it is famous for

A 15th-century marble temple famed for its forest of individually carved pillars.

Folklore & mythology

Tradition links the temple to a patron inspired by a vision of a celestial vehicle, and the design — open on four sides to a central image — is read as an invitation to approach the divine from every direction.

Jain Karnataka · South India

Shravanabelagola

How to reach

Nearest airports Bengaluru and Mysuru; reached by road, with the statue standing atop Vindhyagiri hill.

What to do & see

Climb the rock-cut steps to the colossal monolithic statue of Bahubali (Gommateshwara) and take in the view from the summit.

Rituals & practices

Reverence at the statue is year-round, but the great event is the Mahamastakabhisheka, the ceremonial anointing held once about every twelve years.

What it is famous for

Home to one of the world's tallest monolithic statues, carved from a single block of granite.

Folklore & mythology

The statue honours Bahubali, who in Jain tradition renounced a hard-won kingdom and stood in such long, still meditation that vines are said to have grown around his limbs — an image of complete detachment, recalled in the great anointing ceremony.

Buddhist Bihar · East India

Bodh Gaya

How to reach

Gaya has an airport and railway station a short drive from the Mahabodhi Temple complex.

What to do & see

Sit in meditation beneath the Bodhi tree, circumambulate the Mahabodhi Temple, and visit the monasteries built here by Buddhist countries.

Rituals & practices

Meditation, circumambulation and offerings at the Bodhi tree and the diamond throne (vajrasana) are central.

What it is famous for

The place where the Buddha is believed to have attained enlightenment; a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Folklore & mythology

Tradition holds that Siddhartha Gautama, after long searching, sat beneath a pipal tree here resolved not to rise until he understood the end of suffering — and awakened as the Buddha. A descendant of that Bodhi tree still grows beside the temple.

Buddhist Uttar Pradesh · North India

Sarnath

How to reach

Near Varanasi, served by its airport and railway; Sarnath is a short drive from the city.

What to do & see

Walk the ruins of the Deer Park, see the great Dhamek Stupa, and visit the museum that holds the famous Lion Capital.

Rituals & practices

Quiet reverence, circumambulation of the Dhamek Stupa and visits by monastic communities mark the site.

What it is famous for

Where the Buddha gave his first teaching, 'turning the wheel of the Dharma', and the find-spot of the Lion Capital that became India's state emblem.

Folklore & mythology

At the Deer Park of Isipatana, tradition says, the newly awakened Buddha found his first five companions and taught them the Middle Way and the Four Noble Truths — the moment remembered as the first turning of the wheel of the Dharma.

Buddhist Uttar Pradesh · North India

Kushinagar

How to reach

Kushinagar has an international airport; nearest major railhead Gorakhpur.

What to do & see

Visit the Parinirvana Stupa with its large reclining Buddha and the cremation stupa nearby.

Rituals & practices

Devotees offer robes to the reclining image and gather for quiet remembrance, especially at Buddha Purnima.

What it is famous for

The place where the Buddha is believed to have passed into final nirvana (mahaparinirvana).

Folklore & mythology

Tradition recalls the Buddha's last journey and his passing among the sal trees here, with his final counsel to his followers to strive on with diligence — remembered in the serene reclining image.

Buddhist Madhya Pradesh · Central India

Sanchi

How to reach

Nearest city Bhopal with an airport and railway; Sanchi has a small railway station and is reached by road.

What to do & see

Walk around the Great Stupa and study its four carved gateways (toranas), among the finest early Buddhist art in India.

Rituals & practices

Circumambulation of the Great Stupa is the traditional act of reverence at this now largely archaeological site.

What it is famous for

Home to the Great Stupa begun under Emperor Ashoka in the third century BCE; a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Folklore & mythology

Sanchi is tied to the memory of Ashoka, who after the bloodshed of his Kalinga war turned to the Buddha's path and raised stupas across his realm — and the carved gateways here tell the Buddha's life through symbol rather than figure.

Buddhist Bihar · East India

Rajgir & Nalanda

How to reach

Nearest airport Patna or Gaya; both sites are reached by road, with a railway station at Rajgir.

What to do & see

Climb Vulture Peak (Gridhrakuta) where the Buddha is said to have taught, and walk the vast brick ruins of the Nalanda Mahavihara.

Rituals & practices

Pilgrims meditate on Vulture Peak and walk reverently through the monastic ruins, which many Buddhist traditions honour.

What it is famous for

Rajgir was an early centre of the Buddha's teaching; Nalanda was one of the ancient world's great monastic universities.

Folklore & mythology

Rajgir remembers the Buddha teaching on Vulture Peak and the first Buddhist council held after his passing. Nearby, the Nalanda Mahavihara flourished especially from around the fifth century CE, drawing students from across Asia before its decline centuries later.

Buddhist Arunachal Pradesh · Northeast India

Tawang Monastery

How to reach

A long mountain road from Guwahati or Tezpur (Assam); the nearest airports and railheads are in Assam, with helicopter links in season. High-altitude; permits apply.

What to do & see

Visit one of the largest monasteries in India, set among the eastern Himalayas, and see its great hall and library.

Rituals & practices

Prayer, the turning of prayer wheels and the monastery's festivals mark the devotional life of this Gelug-school community.

What it is famous for

A major Tibetan Buddhist monastery high in the eastern Himalayas.

Folklore & mythology

The monastery is remembered as the birthplace of the sixth Dalai Lama, and its remote mountain setting has long made it a stronghold of Himalayan Buddhist learning and devotion.

Buddhist Himachal Pradesh · North India

Dharamshala & McLeod Ganj

How to reach

Prayer, circumambulation of the temple and attendance at teachings mark the spiritual life of this Tibetan centre.

The home in exile of the 14th Dalai Lama and a hub of Tibetan Buddhism in India.

Since the mid-twentieth century this Himalayan town has become a living centre of Tibetan culture and Buddhism in exile, drawing seekers from around the world to its temples and teachings.

Island tradition remembers Hazrat Ubaidullah as the traveller who, guided by a dream, brought the faith to Lakshadweep and whose tomb on Andrott remains a focus of devotion.

Hindu Andaman & Nicobar Islands · Islands India

Andaman Islands (Port Blair)

How to reach

Reached by flights and ships to Port Blair from Chennai, Kolkata and Visakhapatnam; some areas require permits.

What to do & see

Port Blair and the settled islands have community temples, churches and mosques serving residents; the islands are better known for the Cellular Jail national memorial than for large-scale pilgrimage.

Rituals & practices

Local congregations keep the ordinary rhythms of worship; there is no single ancient pilgrimage centre here on the scale of the mainland sites.

What it is famous for

India's Bay of Bengal island territory, of deep historical significance and growing travel interest.

Folklore & mythology

Rather than ancient pilgrimage legend, the islands carry the modern memory of the freedom struggle, centred on the Cellular Jail — a place of pilgrimage of a different, national kind.
